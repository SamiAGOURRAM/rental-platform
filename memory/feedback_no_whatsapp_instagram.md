Never suggest WhatsApp, Instagram, or similar informal channels for booking or customer interaction. Florence wants a proper platform (custom website with Stripe) that establishes trust and feels premium.

**Why:** Asking people to pay a stranger via informal channels kills trust. The platform IS the product — it needs to feel safe, intuitive, and premium.

**How to apply:** Always recommend proper e-commerce/booking flows with established payment processors. The website should be the single source of truth for all transactions.
